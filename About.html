About

Josie Kwan is a recent Georgia Tech alum with a BS in Literature, Media, and Communication. Her classes focused on media studies, film history, and literature. If she is not outside soaking up the sun you can find her at her local coffee shop or library reading a book. And sometimes she even preforms at the coffee shop's open mics. You can read some of her pieces in the student magazine, North Ave Review which she was a part of while at Tech. Poetry is her favorite way to express herself, but she writes plenty of prose as well.
